Tavla is a fast, privacy-first client for Nextcloud Deck, built for people who want their boards to work as well offline as they do connected.

BOARDS, THE WAY DECK INTENDED
Browse, create and manage boards, lists and cards, with full support for due dates, labels, assignees, attachments, descriptions and archiving. Cards sync straight to your own Nextcloud over WebDAV, no third-party server involved.

BUILT FOR OFFLINE, NOT JUST TOLERANT OF IT
Edits you make without a connection are saved locally and queued for the server, with automatic retries once you're back online. Boards, cards and board cover images are all cached on-device, so Tavla stays fully usable on a plane, in the subway, or anywhere else Nextcloud isn't reachable.

SOLIDTIME INTEGRATION
Link a board, or an individual card, to a Solidtime project and track time directly from Tavla: start and stop a timer from any card, auto-generate Solidtime tasks per card, tag entries by list, and review time logged against a card without leaving the app. Timers keep running safely through app restarts and offline stretches, and sync back to Solidtime the moment you're connected again.

SEE ACROSS YOUR BOARDS, NOT JUST INSIDE ONE
Search finds any card by title or description across every board at once. A calendar view lays out everything due this week or month, lets you jump into any day, and reschedule a card by dragging it to a new date. Board insights show cards completed per week, overdue trends, average time-to-done and your busiest lists, all computed locally from what's already synced.

PART OF THE BARBURASLAB SUITE
Tavla links cards to contacts you manage in Vinci, so a card shows up right on that contact's profile, one more way the suite works better together without ever leaving Nextcloud as the source of truth.

MAKE IT YOURS
System, Light, Dark and OLED Black themes, each board's own color accenting its own screen, adjustable text size, and the DM Serif Display + Inter Tight font pairing shared across the suite.

NO COMPROMISES ON PRIVACY
Tavla talks only to your own Nextcloud. Nothing is sent anywhere else, no accounts to create, no analytics running in the background. It's free, open-source, and built for people who want to own their own data.

FEATURES
- Direct Nextcloud Deck sync over WebDAV, no third-party server
- Full board, list and card management, including labels, assignees, due dates, attachments and archiving
- Checklists and comments that stay in sync with Deck on the web
- Offline edits with a queue: changes made without a connection are saved locally and sent to the server later, with retries
- Cross-board search by card title and description
- Calendar view with drag-to-reschedule
- Board insights: cards finished per week, overdue trend, average time to done, busiest lists
- Solidtime time tracking: per-board project links, per-card overrides, start/stop timers, auto-generated tasks, list tags
- Board cover images and icons, synced through your own Nextcloud
- Linked contacts: link a card to a Vinci contact, visible from both apps
- Local due-date reminders, no push server involved
- Share text or a link from any app straight into a new card
- Optional biometric app lock
- System, Light, Dark and OLED Black themes, adjustable text size
